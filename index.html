WHY IT MATTERS

Small actions, cleaner future.

Electronic waste can contain materials that need careful handling. Reuse working devices and use authorized collection or recycling services for items that are no longer useful.

🌱

Protect Nature

Keep old electronics out of ordinary household waste streams.

🔧

Reuse Devices

Repair or donate usable electronics when it is safe and practical.

🔋

Handle Safely

Do not dismantle batteries or damaged electronic equipment yourself.
